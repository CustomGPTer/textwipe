TextWipe works by text, with nothing online

Where's My Droid and Google's Find Hub are the best-known ways to find, ring or erase a lost Android phone. TextWipe does those jobs by text alone, and adds a warning you can cancel and a wipe of just the folders you choose. This page shows only the rows where TextWipe does more.

What TextWipe does not do

TextWipe has no website to control it from, cannot take photos or track a phone over time, and needs a SIM that can receive texts. A full factory reset on Android 14 and later needs TextWipe set up as the phone's Device Owner. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where the others do more

Find Hub can find a phone that is switched off or offline through Google's network, works from any web browser, and comes built in. Where's My Droid has a web control centre, and its Elite version adds camera pictures, geofencing and location history.
