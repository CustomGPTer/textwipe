Release notes

What changed in each version of TextWipe. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.2 26 November 2026Latest

  • Fixed: the Chosen folders screen closed the app when you opened a storage folder that held a folder you had already chosen.
  • Fixed: the clock and battery icons at the top of the screen could be dark on TextWipe's dark background, or light on its light one. They now follow TextWipe's own theme.
  • Fixed: screens no longer refresh every second in the background, which used battery for no reason.
  • Better: granting permissions no longer asks for your PIN again each time you come back from an Android settings screen TextWipe opened (within two minutes).
  • New: Settings, Check for updates and More apps. Settings now says Developed by Jonathan Jackson ©, and the opening screen says Privacy by design.

Version 1.0.1 25 November 2026

  • Fixed: the Overview and Access screens could close the app when Device Admin was off or was being removed.
  • Fixed: folder access checks could close the app when no main storage was available.

Version 1.0.0 25 November 2026

  • First release. LOCK, RING, LOCATE, WIPE and RESET by text, with your PIN, from any number.
  • A 15-second alarm your PIN can cancel before any wipe or reset; test mode and dry runs; a one-hour lockout after three wrong PINs.
  • Encrypted settings and history, PIN or fingerprint to open the app, and no internet permission.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.2 is called TextWipe.apk. It is 3.8 MB (3,764,031 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

cd5046708631547d77a568a4c5c9ba4afcb3b8762322277c8f639e704e9a7b40

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile TextWipe.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
