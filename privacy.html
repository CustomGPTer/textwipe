Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the TextWipe app for Android (package uk.jj.textwipe) and this website. TextWipe is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • TextWipe has no internet permission. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.
  • It reads incoming texts only to look for a command word and your PIN. Message text and PINs are never saved.
  • The only things that leave your phone are text replies to the number that sent a correct command, and an alert to your trusted contact after three wrong PINs.

What stays on your phone

TextWipe keeps your settings, chosen folders, trusted contact, command history (up to 300 events), and any command that is waiting to run. They are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using keys held in Android's Keystore. Your PIN is kept only as a salted hash. History can include sender numbers, outcomes and the map links that were sent. Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for TextWipe.

Texts and your mobile provider

Commands and replies are ordinary texts, so they pass through your mobile provider and the sender's. Your provider may charge for replies. A map link is plain text; the person reading it needs a map app and data to open it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Clear history with your PIN on the History screen. Uninstalling TextWipe deletes everything it holds. Remove Device Admin first, from TextWipe's Settings or Android's settings.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.
